Turn Waste Into Something Wonderful

Recycling and upcycling can help us create a cleaner, greener and healthier world.

♻️ What is Recycling?

Recycling is the process of collecting and processing waste materials to make new products. It helps reduce waste, save energy and protect natural resources.

🌱 What is Upcycling?

Upcycling means creatively reusing old or unwanted items to make something useful or beautiful. For example, an old tire can become a plant pot.
